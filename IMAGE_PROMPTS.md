# Image prompts (AI fallback)

For channels without a concept image. Generate at 3:2, then put the PNG in
`assets-src/brand/`, run `pnpm images:brand`, and point the channel's `heroImage` /
`cardImage` frontmatter at the new file.

House style for every prompt: *photoreal, Indian city context, blue hour or warm studio
light, coral #FF5B3A accent on the media surface, charcoal and silver tones, 3:2, no real
brand logos, no legible text.*
