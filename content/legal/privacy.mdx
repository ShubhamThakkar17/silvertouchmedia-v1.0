This policy explains what personal data Silver Touch Media collects through this website, why, and the choices you have. It is written to follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

### What we collect

- **Details you give us** in the contact form or the media planner: your name, phone number, and optionally your company, email, city, industry, budget range and message. In the planner we also keep the plan inputs you chose.
- **How you found us:** a first-party cookie records the campaign tags in the link you arrived from (such as utm_source), the referring site and your first page, so we know which campaigns work. We also note whether you used a phone, tablet or computer.
- **Analytics and advertising cookies,** only if you choose "Accept all" in the cookie banner: Google Analytics 4 and Meta Pixel measure visits and campaign results.

We do not ask for financial details, government IDs or passwords.

### Why we use it

- To reply to your enquiry and prepare the media plan you asked for, including emailing you the PDF.
- To follow up about that enquiry by phone, WhatsApp or email.
- To understand which pages and campaigns bring enquiries, so we can improve them.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use your details for unrelated marketing without asking.

### Who processes it for us

Your enquiry is stored in our lead sheet and sent to our team by email. We use service providers to run the site: website hosting, an email delivery service, a spreadsheet for lead records, a bot-protection check on forms, and, only with your consent, analytics and advertising tools. They process data on our instructions.

### How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for as long as we are discussing work with you and for up to 24 months after our last contact, unless you ask us to delete them sooner or the law requires us to keep them longer.

### Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, or withdraw consent at any time. To change cookie choices, use "Cookie settings" at the bottom of any page. To make a request, write to us at the email below; we will reply within 30 days.

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
