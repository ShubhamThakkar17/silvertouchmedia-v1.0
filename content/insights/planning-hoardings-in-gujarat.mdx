A hoarding is the most direct way to own a place, but only if people see it often enough to remember it. Most wasted outdoor money comes from good boards in the wrong places, or one great board standing alone.

### Think in routes

Start from your customer's day. Where do they drive to work, to school, to the mall? Pick one or two of those routes and plan several sites along each, so the same person passes you more than once a day. Three boards on one corridor usually beat ten scattered across a city.

### Know what sizes cost

Current vendor rate cards for Gujarat give a useful sense of scale, before printing, mounting and GST:

- A 20×20 ft city site starts around ₹25,000 a month.
- 40×20 ft sites range from about ₹35,000 to ₹80,000 a month depending on location.
- Large 40×41 ft expressway boards run about ₹80,000 to ₹85,000 a month, and an 81×41 ft board at a toll plaza about ₹1.75 lakh.

Printing and mounting add roughly ₹14 per square foot, which is about ₹23,000 for a 40×41 ft board. Many site owners prefer bookings of three months or more.

### Seven words and one image

Drivers give a hoarding about three seconds. Use one strong image, a short line and your brand. Leave the phone number off unless it is very short; a memorable name or a simple search term works better.

### Prove it ran, then measure what it moved

Insist on dated, geo-tagged photos when the flex goes up and during the campaign. Then watch what outdoor is meant to change: branded search, footfall and enquiries in the catchment around your sites. Pair it with search and social so the attention it creates turns into calls.

Rates change by season and availability, so treat these as a planning guide and ask for live quotes before you book.
