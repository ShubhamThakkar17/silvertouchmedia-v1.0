Hoardings are the most direct way to own a place. A board on the road your customers drive every day builds familiarity without asking for attention, and in many Indian categories, a strong outdoor presence is what makes a brand feel established.

Outdoor has held up better than most traditional media. Real estate is one of its biggest users, and launches, jewellery, education and healthcare lean on it heavily too.

### When hoardings work best

- **Launches and openings**, when you need to look big quickly in one city or corridor.
- **Location-led businesses**: projects, showrooms, hospitals, schools and stores.
- **Highway and expressway reach** for intercity buyers, dealers and factory owners.

### Planning tips

Think in routes, not sites. Keep creative to seven words and one strong image. Add printing and mounting to your budget, and plan at least a month, ideally three.
