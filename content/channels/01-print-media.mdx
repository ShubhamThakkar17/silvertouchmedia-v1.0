Print is the channel people still read slowly. A newspaper arrives at home, gets passed around the family and is read with the first cup of tea. That gives a print ad something most media struggle to buy: time and trust.

India's print market remains one of the largest in the world because of regional-language papers. A Gujarati, Hindi or Marathi daily often reaches households that English media and even social feeds miss, especially in tier 2 and 3 towns.

### When print works best

- **Launches and big announcements**, where a front page signals scale and seriousness.
- **Detailed offers** such as property prices, admission dates or financing terms that need more than five seconds.
- **B2B and investor audiences**, who still read business papers and trade magazines.
- **Local catchments**, using city supplements so you only pay for the area you serve.

### Planning tips

Book fewer, bigger insertions on the right days rather than many small ads. Pair every insertion with a way to respond, and follow up with social and search on the same day to catch the interest it creates.
