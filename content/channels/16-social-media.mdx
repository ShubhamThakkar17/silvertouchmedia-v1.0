Social media is where Indian audiences spend much of their day, and where a brand can be present every day rather than once a campaign. Paid social lets you choose exactly which cities, ages and interests see each message, and measure the result to the rupee.

It works across the funnel: building awareness with video, consideration with content and creators, and enquiries with lead forms and click-to-WhatsApp ads.

### When social works best

- **Lead generation** for real estate, education, healthcare, solar and B2B.
- **Launches**, combining video reach with remarketing.
- **Supporting offline media**, catching people after they see a hoarding, ad or creator post.

### Planning tips

Test several creatives every week and turn off losers quickly. Route every lead to sales within hours, and judge success on cost per qualified enquiry, not clicks.
