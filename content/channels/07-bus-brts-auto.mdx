Transit media takes your brand to people instead of waiting for them to pass a board. Buses, BRTS coaches and autos move through every part of a city all day, which builds frequency at a low cost per contact.

Transit is a large share of India's outdoor market. In cities like Ahmedabad and Surat, BRTS corridors and shelters add structured, high-footfall inventory alongside city buses and autos.

### When transit works best

- **Local and neighbourhood brands**: schools, clinics, retail, real estate and services.
- **City-wide launches** that need presence in every zone, not one junction.
- **Reaching Bharat audiences** in tier 2 and 3 towns, where autos are everywhere.

### Planning tips

Pick routes that pass your stores, sites or campuses. Keep the creative bold enough to read at a glance and in traffic. Combine with a few static sites near your location so moving reach meets a fixed reminder.
