Airports concentrate the audiences many brands most want: business owners, senior professionals and affluent families, in a calm setting with time to look around. Airports are also the largest part of transit media in India.

Formats range from classic backlit panels to digital networks, baggage-belt takeovers and experiential zones where a car or product can be seen up close.

### When airports work best

- **Premium and B2B brands** targeting decision-makers and HNIs.
- **Real estate and jewellery**, especially at regional airports with strong local business traffic.
- **Launches that need stature**, where presence at the airport signals scale.

### Planning tips

Choose terminals and zones by the routes your audience flies. Arrival areas suit city-specific messages; departures suit national ones. Combine with in-flight media for the same traveller from kerb to cabin.
