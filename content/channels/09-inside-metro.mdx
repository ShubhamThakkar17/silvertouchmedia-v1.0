Metro stations give advertisers something roads do not: time. Commuters wait on platforms, walk through concourses and ride for several stops, often on the same route every day. That makes station and coach media ideal for messages that need a second look.

Naming rights take it further, adding a brand to the station's identity on maps, signage and announcements.

### When inside-metro works best

- **Education and coaching**, reaching students and parents on their daily route.
- **BFSI, telecom and services**, with offers that need reading time and a QR code.
- **Local businesses** near a specific station.

### Planning tips

Pick stations by who uses them, not just how many. Repeat the same message across platform, stairs and coaches so commuters meet it several times on one journey.
