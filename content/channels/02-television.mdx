Television is still where most Indian families gather each evening. It puts sight, sound and motion in front of large audiences at the same time, which makes it the fastest way to make a brand feel big and familiar.

Linear TV is flat rather than growing, and younger viewers increasingly watch on connected TVs and phones. That changes how we plan it, not whether it works. For mass categories, family products and anything that needs instant credibility, TV remains hard to replace.

### When TV works best

- **Mass and family categories**: FMCG, auto, BFSI, telecom and jewellery.
- **Regional dominance**: a regional channel mix can make a brand the name everyone knows in its home state.
- **Big moments**: festivals, launches and cricket, when attention is at its peak.

### Planning tips

Plan to reach and frequency, not spot counts. Short 10-second edits stretch a budget well once a longer film has done the storytelling. Always run search and social alongside to catch the interest TV creates.
