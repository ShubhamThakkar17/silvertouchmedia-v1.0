Cinema delivers something rare in modern media: a full, captive audience with the lights off and nothing else to look at. Recall from a good cinema film is high, and the setting lends a brand a sense of occasion.

The audience skews young and urban, which makes cinema a natural fit for launches, lifestyle brands and anything aimed at Gen Z and young professionals. Single-screen networks extend reach into smaller towns.

### When cinema works best

- **Launches timed to big releases**, when footfall peaks.
- **Local businesses near a multiplex**, such as restaurants, clinics, showrooms and new projects.
- **Experiential moments**, combining the screen with foyer activations and sampling.

### Planning tips

Plan around the release calendar, not the calendar month. Combine screen time with a foyer presence and a QR-led offer so the attention turns into action after the film.
