Radio is the companion on the commute and the background in the shop. It costs little per contact and can be bought city by city, which makes it one of the easiest ways for a local brand to build frequency fast.

The voice of a popular RJ carries trust. A live read or show integration often lands harder than a produced spot, because listeners hear it from someone they already like.

### When radio works best

- **Local launches and offers** that need repetition in one or two cities.
- **Retail, healthcare, education and real estate**, where a phone number or store address can be repeated.
- **Supporting OOH and TV**, adding frequency at a low cost per contact.

### Planning tips

Keep scripts simple: one message, one call to action, repeated. Buy morning and evening drive time, and run search alongside so listeners who look you up find you first.
