A familiar face shortens the path to trust. In India, film stars, cricketers and regional icons carry enormous credibility, especially with families and Bharat audiences who may not know your brand yet.

The ambassador is not the media plan. Their value comes from appearing consistently across TV, outdoor, print, social and retail, so every channel borrows the same recognition.

### When ambassadors work best

- **Launches and repositioning**, when you need instant recognition.
- **Trust-led categories**: jewellery, BFSI, healthcare, FMCG and real estate.
- **Regional dominance**, using a local star who means more in one state than a national name.

### Planning tips

Choose for fit before fame. Secure usage rights for every channel and territory you plan to use, and budget for the media that will carry the face, not just the fee.
