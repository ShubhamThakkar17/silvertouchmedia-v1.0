Creators are the new word of mouth. People follow them by choice, trust their recommendations and watch their content to the end, which makes creator marketing one of the fastest-growing parts of Indian advertising.

The biggest shift is regional. Creators who speak Gujarati, Hindi, Marathi, Tamil or Telugu reach audiences in tier 2 and 3 cities that English content and national celebrities often miss.

### When creators work best

- **D2C, FMCG, fashion and beauty**, where seeing a product in use drives purchase.
- **Local businesses**, using city pages and neighbourhood creators.
- **Launches**, with a wave of creators posting in the same week.

### Planning tips

Brief the outcome, not the script. Plan long-term relationships with creators who perform, and set aside budget to boost the best posts as ads.
