A wrapped metro train is impossible to miss. It passes thousands of people on platforms and roads every day and becomes a talking point in its own right, which is why brands use wraps for launches and big statements.

The audience is urban, young and working, exactly the group many brands find hard to reach on TV. Inside, the commute gives riders time to read and scan a QR code.

### When metro wraps work best

- **Launches and rebrands** that need city-wide visibility fast.
- **Urban categories**: tech, telecom, BFSI, education, retail and real estate in metro corridors.
- **Social-first campaigns**, where the wrap is designed to be photographed and shared.

### Planning tips

Book wraps early, since rakes are limited. Combine the exterior with interior panels so the story continues inside, and plan for at least a month to justify production.
