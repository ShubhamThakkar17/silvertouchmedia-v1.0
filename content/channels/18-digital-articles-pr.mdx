PR and digital articles build the credibility that advertising alone cannot. When a buyer, investor or partner searches for your name, the coverage they find shapes whether they trust you enough to call.

Digital publishing has made this more measurable. Releases and articles on news portals rank in search, carry backlinks and can be repurposed across social, sales decks and ads.

### When PR works best

- **Launches, funding, expansions and milestones** that deserve news.
- **B2B, BFSI, healthcare and real estate**, where trust closes deals.
- **Relaunches and repositioning**, to change what people find when they search.

### Planning tips

Lead with a real story. Pair earned coverage with a few paid placements so key messages are guaranteed to appear, and always label sponsored content clearly.
