Search is where demand shows itself. When someone types a category, a city and a need into Google, they are telling you they are ready to act. Search and performance campaigns put your brand in front of them at that moment, and measure every rupee against enquiries and sales.

Search also makes the rest of the plan work harder. Hoardings, TV, PR and creators all send people to search for your name; being there to catch them is how awareness turns into leads.

### When search works best

- **Lead-led businesses**: real estate, education, healthcare, solar, B2B and services.
- **Any campaign with offline media**, to capture the searches it creates.
- **Established brands** defending their name against competitors.

### Planning tips

Build one landing page per offer and track every call and form. Start with tightly matched keywords, add more as data comes in, and review search terms weekly.
