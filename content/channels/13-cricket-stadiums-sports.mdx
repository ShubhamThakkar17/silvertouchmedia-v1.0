Cricket is India's biggest shared ritual, and the sports economy keeps growing on its back. Stadium media puts your brand inside that moment, both in front of fans at the ground and in the broadcast frames watched at home.

Beyond the IPL and international cricket, state leagues, kabaddi, football and corporate tournaments offer more affordable ways for regional brands to borrow the energy of sport.

### When sports works best

- **Mass and youth brands** in FMCG, telecom, auto, BFSI and fantasy or gaming.
- **National launches** timed to big tournaments.
- **Regional brands** sponsoring state and city leagues where their customers play and watch.

### Planning tips

Value the broadcast, not just the stadium. Pair on-ground boards with TV or streaming spots and match-day social so the same fan sees you several times in one evening.
