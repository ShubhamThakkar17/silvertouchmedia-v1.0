Indian Railways carries a huge share of the country on the move, and train media reaches people when they have time: seated, often for hours, on routes that link big cities to smaller towns.

Premium trains such as Tejas and Vande Bharat add onboard screens, headrests, meal trays and in-train magazines that reach professionals and business owners. Exterior wraps and station media extend visibility to everyone along the route.

### When railways work best

- **Corridor brands**, such as a Gujarat business selling into Mumbai or a regional chain along one route.
- **Bharat reach** for FMCG, BFSI, telecom and government campaigns.
- **Business travellers** on premium trains for B2B and real estate.

### Planning tips

Plan for the three-month minimum and for approval time. Combine onboard media for depth with station media for breadth, and use coupons or QR codes to track response.
