In-flight media reaches a captive, premium audience: frequent flyers sitting still for an hour or more with few distractions. Headrests and meal trays sit in direct line of sight for the entire flight.

It is a niche channel, best used to reach decision-makers and affluent travellers rather than mass audiences, and it pairs naturally with airport branding.

### When in-flight works best

- **Premium products and services**: real estate, luxury, BFSI and hospitality.
- **B2B brands** targeting business travellers on key routes.
- **Sampling**, putting a product in hand at the right moment.

### Planning tips

Pick formats that stay in view the whole flight, such as headrests and trays. Run at least a month to reach repeat flyers, and link it to airport presence on the same routes.
