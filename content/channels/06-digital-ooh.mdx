Digital OOH puts the flexibility of online advertising onto real-world screens. Malls, office towers, residential societies, airports and busy junctions now carry screens that can be bought by the slot or programmatically by audience and time.

Digital is the fastest-growing part of outdoor. It lets a brand change its message by time of day, react to weather or cricket scores, and test creative without reprinting a single flex.

### When DOOH works best

- **Premium urban audiences** in malls, offices and gated communities.
- **Time-sensitive offers**, such as lunch deals, weekend sales or launch countdowns.
- **Campaigns that need to start fast** or change often.

### Planning tips

Day-part your buy to when your audience is actually there. Make several creative versions, and add a QR code with a clear offer so screen time can be measured.
